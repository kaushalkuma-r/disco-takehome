## Where these ads run
Disco is a commerce media network. Ads appear on the checkout, order-confirmation and post-purchase pages of the publishers in the catalog. The reader has just bought something from that publisher, is in a buying frame of mind, and will give an ad about two seconds. Relevance to what they just bought and to who they are is everything; generic ads are ignored.

## The catalog
20 publishers (consumer commerce: apparel, wellness, pet, home, grocery, beauty, beverages, meal kits, instant delivery). Each has a category, subcategories, audience (age band, gender split, top geos, income tier), monthly impressions, average order value (AOV) and a qualitative note written by the ops team. 10 shopper personas describe the kinds of people who shop on these publishers: category affinities, price sensitivity, messaging preferences and disinterests.

## Price tiers (USD, typical order)
budget < 25 · mid 25–80 · premium 80–300 · luxury 300+. A subscription is priced by its monthly charge.

## House rules
- Never invent facts about the advertiser: ingredients, materials, certifications, prices, numbers, guarantees, awards. Use only what the brief says; when something is unknown, say so.
- Publisher and persona ids must come from the lists you are given (pub_001…pub_020, persona_001…persona_010).
- Write for the advertiser: plain English, specific, no marketing-speak, no ad-tech jargon.
